# Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models are AI models trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like text.

Key concepts:
- Tokenization: Breaking text into smaller units
- Attention mechanism: Focusing on relevant parts of input
- Pre-training: Learning from large corpora
- Fine-tuning: Adapting to specific tasks

Examples:
- GPT-4 (OpenAI)
- Claude (Anthropic)
- LLaMA (Meta)
