# Ethical Considerations in AI

As AI becomes more prevalent, ethical considerations become increasingly important.

Key ethical concerns:
1. Bias: AI systems can perpetuate existing biases
2. Privacy: Data collection and usage concerns
3. Job displacement: Impact on employment
4. Autonomy: Decision-making power of AI systems
5. Transparency: Understanding how AI makes decisions

Principles for ethical AI:
- Fairness
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Privacy protection
