# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn like humans.

Key characteristics of AI systems:
- Learning: The ability to acquire new knowledge from experience
- Reasoning: Using logic to solve problems
- Perception: Understanding sensory inputs
- Language: Processing and understanding natural language

AI can be categorized into:
1. Narrow AI: Designed for specific tasks
2. General AI: Hypothetical AI with human-like intelligence
3. Superintelligence: AI that surpasses human intelligence
